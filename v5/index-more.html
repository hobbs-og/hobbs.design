More on how I work.

My role is to bring structure and direction to ambiguity. Helping teams define what actually matters, make tradeoffs explicit, and turn complexity into something people can understand and use. I care about how things feel as much as how they function, not in a surface-level way, but in how clarity, hierarchy, and interaction design shape trust and behavior over time. The goal is making sure it holds up when it ships, scales, and gets used in the real world.

What that means in practice

Design systems that hold up under pressure

Create patterns and foundations that scale without losing clarity or cohesion.

Build accessibility in from the start

Accessibility isn't a layer. It's how I structure decisions, flows, and interactions.

Define and refine what actually matters

Cut through noise, shape direction, and make complex products feel clear and usable.

Turn constraints into workable solutions

Use product, technical, and business limitations to create practical, shippable outcomes.

Find the signal in noisy products

Simplify complexity and bring focus to products that have grown hard to understand.
