01 — How I work

Structure is the product, before the pixels are.

Most of what I get called in for has less to do with new ideas and more to do with structure: no agreed vocabulary for what a component is, no plan for what happens when a token changes upstream, nobody responsible for a pattern once it leaves Figma.

I build that structure. Token architecture, component APIs, and the governance that keeps a system from drifting apart after it ships. Accessibility gets built in at that same layer, not added at the end.
