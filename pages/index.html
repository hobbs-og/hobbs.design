I do my best work where nobody agrees yet.

The hardest product problems start with competing priorities, unclear ownership, inherited systems, technical limits and teams pulling in different directions. My job is to get those pieces on the table, name the tradeoffs and land on a decision the team can build.

Then I build that decision into the system, so nobody has to make it again. I work across product, design and engineering, and I care as much about how something gets built and maintained as how it feels to use. I can't make the complexity go away, but I can make it workable.
