01 — What I solve

I bring structure to complexity.

The hardest product problems rarely start with a blank canvas. They start with competing priorities, unclear ownership, inherited systems, technical constraints, and teams pulling in different directions.

I bring those pieces into focus. I help teams understand what actually matters, make the tradeoffs explicit, and turn complexity into decisions and systems that hold up beyond the design file.

I work across product, design, and engineering as one delivery system, not three separate disciplines. That means thinking about the experience, the patterns underneath it, and how the thing actually gets built, shipped, and maintained.

The goal isn't to make complexity look simpler, it's to make it workable.
