You can't solve
every product problem
with process. Some of it is taste.

I focus on product work
where clarity has to be created.

Most of the time, the problem isn't a lack of ideas, but a lack of clarity. Too many directions, not enough alignment, and no shared sense of what actually matters. I thrive at the intersection of user behavior, technical constraints, and business goals…where things aren't obvious and rarely line up cleanly.

My role is to bring structure to that ambiguity. To make decisions visible, simplify what's gotten complicated, and move products forward in a way that holds up when they're actually used.
