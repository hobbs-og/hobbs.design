Editorial
Design.

Marvel vs. DC is an editorial design piece that compares the two iconic comic book franchises and their cinematic universes to determine who does it best. comparison of how the respective comic book franchises are projected onto the big screen. The visual approach blends the classic comic book aesthetic with the sleek, modern style of their blockbuster movies, creating a design that bridges past and present.

Comic Book Influence

Classic styled comic book panels served as the inspiration for the large visual treatments. Rather than use run-of-the-mill studio provided imagery, we decided to tie them into the comic book world by converting them into panels that utilized multiple characters from both brands.
